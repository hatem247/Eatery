Patron's Unanimous Choice

Classic Beef Burger

EGP 150 inclusive of taxes

Our crown jewel and undisputed crowd favorite. We start with a custom blend of prime Angus chuck and brisket, ground in-house every morning. Searing it on our volcanic stone grill locks in natural juices while imparting an irresistible smoky char.

Layered generously with 18-month aged sharp cheddar, slow-simmered balsamic sweet onions, vine-ripened tomatoes, and our secret smoked-paprika emulsion, all cradled inside a cloud-soft golden brioche bun. Served alongside twice-cooked sea salt fries and house pickle spear.
